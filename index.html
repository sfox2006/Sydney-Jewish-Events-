About

What this is

Jewish community events in Sydney: Shabbat, services, learning, culture, and more, in person, hybrid, and online. Events are checked against each organiser's own page.

Window
The list and calendar show events from today through the next 31 days, in Sydney time. A multi-day event is listed on every day it runs, with a Day N of M badge.
Sources
Synagogues and Jewish organisations across Sydney, including The Great Synagogue in the CBD, Central Synagogue in Bondi Junction, Emanuel Synagogue in Woollahra, Shalom Collective, the NSW Jewish Board of Deputies, the Zionist Council of NSW, Sydney Jewish Museum, JewishCare, Maccabi NSW, and the Jewish International Film Festival at Ritz Cinemas in Randwick. Details are verified against the organiser's own pages.
Unknown
Anything that cannot be confirmed is shown as Unknown. Unknown movement stays Unknown and is not grouped with a denomination. Unknown cost is listed on its own and is never treated as free. Unknown audience is listed on its own.
